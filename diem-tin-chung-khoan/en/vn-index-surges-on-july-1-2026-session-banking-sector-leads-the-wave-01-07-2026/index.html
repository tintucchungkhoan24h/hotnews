VN-Index Surges on July 1, 2026 Session: Banking Sector Leads the Wave

📅 01/07/2026 • 🇺🇸 ENGLISH • Tin Tức Chứng Khoán 24h
Entering the opening trading session of July, the Vietnamese stock market witnessed a strong boom as the VN-Index continuously broke through resistance levels due to positive widespread capital flow. The consensus from banking stocks along with the recovery of technology shares brought an optimistic sentiment, lighting up opportunities for even new investors entering the market.

Macro Analysis & Market Sentiment

The Vietnamese stock market entered July 2026 with widespread optimism after a challenging period of accumulation and testing. Positive macroeconomic information regarding the Q2 earnings forecasts of listed companies provided a solid foundation for investor sentiment. In particular, expectations for the VN-Index to reach new historical milestones thanks to improvements in trading infrastructure, including the proposal for midday trading, activated capital that was waiting on the sidelines. Although order matching liquidity remained moderate at times due to sellers' supply-hoarding psychology, the clear improvement in scores indicated that proactive buying demand completely overwhelmed. The biggest highlight reinforcing the upward trend was the surprising reversal to net buying by foreign investors right in the first session of the month, ending the previous continuous withdrawal streak and alleviating the psychological pressure that had weighed on the market for some time.

Sector & Stock Performance

Capital flow during the session showed significant concentration in the "king" sectors: banking and securities, acting as drivers that pushed VN30 past important psychological resistance levels. The information technology stock group continued to maintain its breakout leading position in terms of return on performance, while the oil and gas group recorded explosive profit growth, typified by BSR with expected outstanding Q2 business results due to high oil prices. Conversely, slight localized selling pressure appeared in some real estate and construction stocks following news that a contractor for Long Thanh airport temporarily suspended its Chairman of the Board. During the trading session, several prominent stocks recorded notable movements, including major banking stocks supporting the index such as VCB up 1.8%, BID up 2.2%, CTG up 2.5%, alongside the surge of technology stock FPT up 3.1% and retail giant MSN up 1.5% after many securities companies simultaneously raised their 2026 profit expectations.

Trends & Recommendations

Technically, the VN-Index's sustained rally and breakthrough of short-term resistance areas indicate that the recovery trend is increasingly solidifying. However, the market is expected to enter a more challenging and differentiated phase as companies gradually announce their Q2 2026 financial reports. Investors are advised to maintain a balanced portfolio, avoiding the psychology of chasing rallies during hot uptrends. Instead, capital should be focused on sectors with strong fundamentals and clear profit growth prospects such as banking, oil and gas, information technology, and consumer goods, while actively managing risks against potential technical fluctuations that may occur at overhead resistance levels.
