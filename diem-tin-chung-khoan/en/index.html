Stock Market on 16/07/2026: Bottom-fishing Capital Activated

📅 16/07/2026 12:17 • 🇺🇸 ENGLISH • Tin Tức Chứng Khoán 24h
After a series of deep downward corrections pushing the index back to attractive price levels, the stock market on 16/07/2026 recorded clear positive signals as bottom-fishing capital was officially activated. The return of demand at key support levels not only helped stabilize investor sentiment, especially for new individual investors (F0s), but also opened up recovery expectations for the short-term trend amidst significantly improved liquidity.

Macroeconomic Analysis & Market Sentiment

The domestic stock market is going through a challenging period after the VN-Index corrected down by about 150 points from its short-term peak. This sharp decline has brought the valuations of many stock groups to relatively attractive levels, stimulating the sentiment of domestic capital to seek out undervalued assets. On the macroeconomic front, investor sentiment is torn between gradually revealed Q2 business results, which show mixed bright and dark colors, along with exchange rate pressure and continuous net selling by foreign investors. However, the market's deep retreat also simultaneously opens up a new equilibrium point. The appearance of active bottom-fishing capital at low price levels indicates that pessimistic sentiment has somewhat dissipated, giving way to expectations for a more solid technical recovery as risk factors are gradually priced in.

Sector & Stock Developments

Deep differentiation occurred on the trading board as capital tended to converge into sectors with underlying fundamental support or those that had just received information about portfolio restructuring. A notable highlight is the official inclusion of two quality newcomers, Masan Consumer (MCH) and TCBS, into the VN30 index basket from early August. Although current foreign ETF funds are buying net insignificantly or are even trending to net sell most stocks in their portfolios, MCH's presence in the VN30 basket is expected to significantly expand its ability to attract capital from large institutions in the medium and long term. Conversely, pressure from foreign investors remained high as they net sold nearly 1,000 billion VND, heavily dumping some hot stocks that had previously surged.

During the session, some stocks recorded notable movements. PNJ shares attracted attention after VinaCapital made new moves to restructure its portfolio following incidents related to the diamond segment. In the transport sector, Hai An Transport and Stevedoring (HAH) announced a plan to issue 3.5 million ESOP shares, reflecting efforts to retain key personnel but also causing mixed reactions regarding dilution concerns. Technically, the market witnessed strong polarization in a K-shaped pattern, with more than 1/3 of the stocks on the exchange having broken below the lows established during the 2025 tariff period, showing the harshness of the market and that capital truly prioritizes businesses with healthy financial foundations, such as F88 proactively settling a bond lot ahead of schedule, or high-dividend-paying businesses like a member of Sonadezi finalizing rights to pay a 4,500 VND/share dividend.

Trends & Recommendations

Overall, the short-term trend of the VN-Index is gradually forming a short-term bottom after a deep decline. The active participation of bottom-fishing capital is a positive technical signal, however, to confirm a sustainable uptrend, the market needs more follow-through explosive sessions with convincing liquidity and consensus from leading stock groups such as banking, securities, and steel. Investors, especially F0s, are advised to maintain a safe portfolio proportion, avoid euphoric buy chases during short-term technical recovery rallies. Portfolio restructuring should focus on stocks with good fundamental foundations, positive forecasted Q2 business results, and those that have discounted to strong support zones, while closely observing foreign investor trading activities and exchange rate developments in subsequent sessions.
