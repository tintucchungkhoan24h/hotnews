The stock market on 10/07/2026 recorded strong tug-of-war movements as cash flow tended to consolidate and overall market liquidity dropped significantly. Although a cautious sentiment prevailed, opportunities to accumulate high-quality stocks still emerged for individual investors, especially F0 investors seeking a solid foundation from businesses with good fundamentals.

Macro Analysis & Market Sentiment

The domestic and international macroeconomic context poses many challenges to investor sentiment. According to experts, although interest rates remain low, this is no longer the sole factor determining the trend of VN-Index in the coming period; it largely depends on the actual profit growth of businesses. VN-Index is not expected to surpass the 2000-point threshold in 2026 as capital withdrawal pressure from foreign funds remains. For instance, three major foreign ETFs hold over USD 1.3 billion in Vietnamese stocks, with Fubon ETF, despite having the best performance, experiencing the strongest capital outflow. Additionally, the impending strong sell-off of banking and real estate stocks by ETFs with a scale of nearly 10,000 billion VND has created significant psychological pressure on the market, causing cash flow to shift to a defensive state and severely declining liquidity.

Sector Developments & Stocks

Market cash flow showed deep differentiation and a tendency to concentrate on certain sectors with unique stories. The fertilizer stock group, after a short period of excitement, is facing long-term challenges in terms of profit margins. Conversely, the banking stock group continued to be a focal point as banks paid over 48,000 billion VND in cash dividends, notably BIDV which is about to pay over 3,200 billion VND in cash to shareholders. The shift in cash flow also reflected strong restructuring activities at some large enterprises, such as PNJ seeking to expand abroad amid the domestic diamond crisis, or changes in high-level personnel at An Binh Securities as the daughter of tycoon Vu Van Tien withdrew from her key position. Foreign investors continued their vigorous net selling, focusing on Bluechip stocks in the VN30 basket, preventing the overall index from successfully breaking out.

Trends & Recommendations

In the short term, VN-Index is forecast to continue its accumulation phase and retest lower support levels as the portfolio restructuring pressure from foreign funds shows no signs of abating. Investors are advised to maintain a safe portfolio weighting, avoiding FOMO buying during technical rebound phases. This is an opportune time to screen and focus on 8 notable businesses in the second half of the year as recommended by SSI, especially those with stable cash flow, a consistent history of paying cash dividends, and less vulnerability to the foreign net selling wave. Closely monitoring domestic cash flow movements and system liquidity will be key to determining the optimal disbursement timing.

Stock Market on July 10, 2026: VN-Index Strives to Maintain Momentum

📅 10/07/2026 12:05 • 🇺🇸 ENGLISH • Tin Tức Chứng Khoán 24h
The stock market in the most recent session saw intense tug-of-war as cash flow clearly diversified across sectors. Despite increased profit-taking pressure on some large-cap stocks, domestic capital quietly supported the market, creating valuable real-world experience opportunities for new investors.

Macro Analysis & Market Sentiment

Market sentiment in the most recent session was influenced by a mix of domestic and international macroeconomic information. Investor attention is focused on the portfolio restructuring activities of large-scale foreign ETFs, which implicitly creates a certain caution in new disbursements. Capital flow in the market is no longer evenly distributed but has begun to cluster into stock groups with their own specific stories or those expected to have positive business results in the second half of the year. Although selling pressure from foreign investors remains, the bright spot is that domestic capital and the sentiment of individual investors have maintained calm, helping the VN-Index avoid panic and continue to accumulate within a narrow range.

Sectoral & Stock Developments

Strong diversification occurred on the trading board as capital flow showed clear shifts. Banking and real estate stock groups are facing restructuring pressure from ETFs with a scale of nearly 10,000 billion VND, significantly narrowing the upward momentum of these groups. Conversely, the fertilizer stock group experienced short-term excitement but still faces many challenges in the medium term. Notably, PNJ stock continues to bear strong selling pressure from foreign investors, negatively affecting the overall index. Some large enterprises like BIDV announced plans to pay large cash dividends, which partially provided a psychological boost for shareholders. The concentration of capital into selected blue-chip stocks indicates that large capital is prioritizing defensive and safety factors over speculative trading.

Trends & Recommendations

In subsequent trading sessions, the VN-Index is forecast to continue its tug-of-war state and test important psychological resistance levels. The inability of the index to break out strongly indicates that the market needs clearer macroeconomic drivers, especially the Q2 business results of listed companies. Investors should maintain caution and avoid FOMO-driven buying during hot rallies. Restructuring portfolios by prioritizing stocks with strong fundamentals, stable cash flow, and less sensitivity to exchange rate or interest rate fluctuations will be a reasonable strategy during this period. Closely monitoring the actions of foreign funds and proprietary trading capital is also a key factor for timely risk management decisions.
