📅 11/10/2026 08:08 • 🇺🇸 ENGLISH • Tin Tức Chứng Khoán 24h
The stock market last week witnessed a deep divergence in the Real Estate sector, as domestic cash flow struggled to support the index against strong capital outflows from foreign investors. While some industry leaders faced record net selling pressure, stocks with specific catalysts still found strong growth momentum.

Prominent Opportunities

Amid general market fluctuations, the Real Estate and Retail stock groups still showed notable bright spots. Most remarkable was the breakout of NVL, which made a spectacular turnaround, attracting large cash flows despite the general cautious trend. This positive development shows that domestic investor confidence is gradually returning to enterprises with clear restructuring efforts.

Along with the rise of the Real Estate group, PNJ also provided a major boost to the market by hitting its ceiling price in a key trading session. The strong concentration of cash flow into industry leaders like PNJ and NVL reflects a strategy of seeking safety and recovery potential from core business results. Active demand at lower price levels helped these tickers stay in the green, creating positive sentiment for related stocks.

Developments Warranting Caution

In contrast to the excitement in a few individual stocks, net selling pressure from foreign investors is becoming the biggest obstacle to the index's recovery. The focus of this selling wave centered on VHM, with a net sell value exceeding 9,000 billion VND, putting direct pressure on the large-cap group. The negative synergy from VHM and VIC dragged the VN-Index down, making investor sentiment more cautious than ever in the face of unpredictable international capital movements.

Additionally, the market recorded sudden block transactions from foreign investors for MCH, with a value of over 9,600 billion VND. In total, foreign investors had a record net selling week of more than 13,500 billion VND in the early days of October. This development not only puts pressure on the index points but also raises big questions about the capital shift trends of large investment funds in the short term, forcing individual investors to maintain a tight defensive stance.

Waiting for a Breakout Signal

Although current stock valuations are considered attractive compared to history, reality shows that this is still not enough to trigger a strong return of large cash flows. A wait-and-see sentiment for a clearer confirmation signal on macro trends is pervading the market. The VN-Index is currently moving in a narrow range with liquidity maintained at an average level, reflecting a fierce tug-of-war between buyers and sellers at important support zones.

During this period, stocks like VIC and VHM are playing a role in regulating the index with opposing movements, causing the market to lack the consensus necessary to break out. Investors are particularly interested in the ability of domestic cash flow to absorb foreign selling. As long as the net selling pressure shows no signs of cooling down, the market is likely to continue its state of accumulation and strong divergence between sectors.

Outlook & Perspective

The outlook for the Real Estate industry in the coming time will largely depend on the speed of legal unblocking and the ability of enterprises to access capital. Cash flow is predicted to continue to be strictly selective, prioritizing tickers with clean land banks and healthy financial structures. For the VN-Index, maintaining psychological support levels will be an important prerequisite to attract sidelined cash flow back in the final quarter of the year.

Investors should prioritize portfolio risk management, avoid chasing prices during euphoric sessions, and take advantage of adjustments to restructure into stocks with good internal strength. Short-term trends may still be influenced by inter-market factors, but in the long term, economic recovery will be the main growth driver for the Vietnamese stock market.
