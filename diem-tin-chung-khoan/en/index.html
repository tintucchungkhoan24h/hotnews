This information was announced at a recent working session between the Ministry of Planning and Investment and several international organizations and businesses regarding the development of Vietnam's Semiconductor Industry Action Plan.

"Golden" Opportunity for Vietnam

At the working session, Mr. Pham Tan Cong, President of the Vietnam Chamber of Commerce and Industry (VCCI), stated that leading global chip manufacturers are trending towards shifting their supply chains to various countries, and Vietnam is one of the most potential options to become a global semiconductor manufacturing hub.

According to Mr. Cong, with a young, abundant workforce, Vietnam has a "golden" opportunity to become a world semiconductor center.

Amid increasingly complex trade tensions between major economies, developing the semiconductor industry will help Vietnam enhance its technological self-reliance, improve its position in the global value chain, and create new business opportunities, contributing to sustainable economic growth.

To develop the semiconductor industry, Mr. Cong recommended that the Ministry of Planning and Investment develop a specific Action Plan, with superior preferential policies regarding capital, land, administrative procedures, and human resource training.

Of these, human resource training for the semiconductor industry needs to be prioritized and implemented proactively and systematically starting now.

Additionally, it is necessary to prioritize the development of chip design to increase added value, and at the same time, create a favorable environment to attract foreign investors into Vietnam's semiconductor supply chain.

Concurring with this view, Mr. Le Duy Thanh, Chairman of the People's Committee of Vinh Phuc province, affirmed that Vinh Phuc would provide maximum support for establishing the semiconductor industry in the province.

Vinh Phuc's leadership also proposed establishing a "special zone" for semiconductor technology development with outstanding policies to attract investment, research, and development.

The goal of Vietnam's Semiconductor Industry Action Plan is to make Vietnam a crucial part of the global semiconductor supply chain, achieving a semiconductor market capitalization of $1 trillion by 2030.

To achieve this goal, Vietnam needs to attract and develop large and small semiconductor enterprises, both domestic and foreign, and build a comprehensive semiconductor ecosystem, from design, manufacturing, testing, to packaging.

Prospects for Vietnam's Semiconductor Industry Development

Recently, President Nguyen Xuan Phuc also affirmed that Vietnam would create all favorable conditions for foreign investors to build semiconductor chip manufacturing plants.

The Vietnamese government has also identified semiconductor industry development as one of its top priorities in the socio-economic development strategy for the 2021-2030 period.

Currently, Vietnam has approximately 5,000 engineers working in the semiconductor industry, a modest number compared to the actual market demand. This workforce needs to be significantly strengthened through training programs and international cooperation.

Several major corporations have invested and are investing in Vietnam, such as Intel with its chip assembly and testing factory, Amkor Technology with its semiconductor material manufacturing factory, and Samsung with its semiconductor component manufacturing factory. These are positive signals indicating the development potential of Vietnam's semiconductor industry.

Vietnam's deeper participation in the global semiconductor supply chain not only brings economic benefits but also helps enhance national technological capacity, creating high-tech products "made in Vietnam".

However, alongside opportunities, Vietnam's semiconductor industry also faces many challenges such as fierce competition from other countries, high capital requirements, complex technology, and reliance on the global market. To overcome these challenges, close coordination among the Government, businesses, and training organizations is needed.
