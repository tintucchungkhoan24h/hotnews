📅 17/10/2026 08:08 • 🇺🇸 ENGLISH • Tin Tức Chứng Khoán 24h
The stock market has recorded positive signs from the Real Estate stock group as smart money begins to show signs of a strong return. As difficulty-removing policies gradually permeate reality, investors are expecting a new growth cycle for industry-leading enterprises.

Outstanding Opportunities

In the context of the general market striving to find a balance point, Real Estate stocks with good fundamentals have quickly attracted demand. The highlight is VHM (Vinhomes) with sudden trading volume and great interest from foreign funds. According to experts, "stable business cash flow and abundant land funds are the main drivers" helping this ticker maintain its leading position. The deployment of new mega-urban projects not only ensures short-term revenue but also strengthens shareholder confidence in sustainable growth potential.

In addition, DXG also recorded positive developments as the secondary real estate market warms up. Demand for this stock increased thanks to expectations of legal unblocking at key projects. The recovery of the real housing segment is creating a major competitive advantage for enterprises with the capacity to implement projects quickly. Investor sentiment towards this stock group is currently quite optimistic, reflected through sessions of wide-range gains accompanied by significantly improved liquidity.

Developments Warranting Caution

Contrary to the green across the industry leaders, profit-taking pressure and cautious sentiment still surround stocks with financial structure issues. NVL is the ticker suffering the strongest selling pressure in the negative group as concerns about debt have not been completely eliminated. Quoting from analytical reports, "bond maturity pressure remains a major barrier to the recovery" of the enterprise. Despite restructuring efforts, the slower-than-expected progress in resolving legal bottlenecks has kept large cash flows from returning enthusiastically.

Similarly, DIG also witnessed a significant decrease as speculative cash flow withdrew after a hot growth phase. Fluctuations from the bond market and leadership changes at some peer enterprises have created a domino effect, making investors more cautious. Net selling pressure from foreign investors on these tickers is causing short-term fluctuations, requiring shareholders to have a strict risk management strategy and not to over-rely on financial leverage during this period.

Waiting for Breakthrough Signals

In a neutral state, VIC and PDR are showing an accumulation trend around strong support zones. For VIC, stock price movements are in a tug-of-war as investors wait for clearer information from the business results of supporting segments. Many opinions suggest that this ticker "needs more confirmation signals from foreign investors before breaking out" of its current price base. Maintaining average liquidity indicates that both buyers and sellers are in an observation state, waiting for a push strong enough from the general market.

This group of stocks plays an important role in maintaining the rhythm of the VN-Index, preventing the market from falling deeply. This sideways trend may last until the next quarterly financial reports are officially announced. This is a phase of cash flow filtering, where institutional investors are silently restructuring portfolios to prepare for the next growth phase when macro factors become more favorable.

Analysis & Outlook

Based on data from Dragon Capital, current market valuations have reflected most interest rate risks and are returning to an attractive zone equivalent to the Covid bottom period. The outlook for the Real Estate industry in the final months of the year depends heavily on the speed of lending rate reductions and the absorption capacity of the housing market. Experts forecast that cash flow will continue to differentiate, prioritizing enterprises with clean land funds and transparent financial capacity. Investors should focus on tickers with individual growth stories and completed legal frameworks for projects opening for sale.

Banking Industry: The $5.4 Billion Capital Raising Race and the Appeal of Domestic Cash Flow

📅 17/10/2026 08:02 • 🇺🇸 ENGLISH • Tin Tức Chứng Khoán 24h
Banking stocks are becoming the market focus as plans for a massive $5.4 billion capital raising from major banks are revealed. Amidst technical adjustments of the VN-Index, the divergence of cash flow among the 'king' stocks is opening up interesting investment scenarios for the end of the year.

Outstanding Opportunities

Smart money is showing signs of returning to banking stocks, led by expectations of a capital increase wave and credit quota expansions. According to reports, at least 8 banks are preparing to implement capital raising plans with a total scale of up to $5.4 billion. This is seen as a 'new race' among giants to strengthen their Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) and create growth space for the new economic cycle. Among them, VPB is emerging as a bright spot as it is continuously being accumulated by proprietary trading desks of securities firms. Experts commented: 'The continuous accumulation of VPB by proprietary traders shows the confidence of domestic financial institutions in the valuation foundation and recovery story of this bank'.

Besides VPB, tickers like TCB and MBB are also maintaining heat due to expected positive Q3 business results. Active demand appearing at support price zones shows that investor sentiment has gradually stabilized after fluctuations. The shift of cash flow from speculative sectors to stocks with good fundamentals like banking is creating a solid support for the general index, while opening up disbursement opportunities for medium and long-term goals.

Developments Warranting Caution

Contrary to the excitement of domestic cash flow, net selling pressure from foreign investors remains a tough puzzle for market sentiment. Most notably, MSB has been continuously 'offloaded' for hundreds of billions of VND by foreign investors in recent sessions. This development not only puts pressure on the market but also raises doubts about the short-term trend of this stock. Some experts observed that 'a banking stock being offloaded by foreign investors for hundreds of billions even in a session where the VN-Index rises is a signal that requires special attention regarding shareholder structure'.

Not only MSB, profit-taking pressure also spread to other tickers like STB as foreign investors maintained a net selling streak across many consecutive sessions. Large funds restructuring portfolios and withdrawing capital from some banking stocks that have increased capital strongly in the past is creating significant resistance. Investors need to carefully observe important support levels, avoiding chasing prices during technical recoveries when supply pressure from foreigners shows no signs of cooling down.

Waiting for a Breakout Signal

In a tug-of-war state, industry leaders like VCB and BID are playing the role of index regulators with relatively neutral movements. Cash flow in these tickers is mainly for rhythm-keeping, with no strong breakthroughs in trading volume yet. The general market sentiment currently is 'waiting for a deeper discount' or clearer confirmation signals from the general trend of the VN-Index before making large disbursement decisions.

This caution is well-founded as the market lacks sufficiently strong supporting information to break out of psychological resistance zones. Large-cap stocks are moving sideways in a narrow range, reflecting a positive accumulation state but also potential risks if cash flow does not return soon. Investors are prioritizing observing supply-demand dynamics at important score milestones to determine whether this is a rest stop or the beginning of a deeper correction.

Outlook & Perspective

In the long term, the banking industry still possesses many sustainable growth drivers thanks to economic recovery and high credit demand at the end of the year. Banks actively increasing capital not only helps improve financial capacity but is also a signal of readiness for a new growth cycle. However, in the short term, divergence will continue strongly based on asset quality and risk management capabilities of each bank.

The appropriate strategy now is to prioritize stocks with clear capital increase stories, supported by domestic cash flow (especially proprietary trading), and having breakthrough Q3 business results. Monitoring foreign investor moves and liquidity developments will be the key for investors to optimize profits and manage risks effectively in this volatile market phase.
