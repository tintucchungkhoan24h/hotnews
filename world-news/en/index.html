This hawkish stance has sent mortgage rates surging and put immense pressure on Asian markets. For Vietnam, this strengthens the USD, complicating the State Bank of Vietnam''s efforts to maintain exchange rate stability without draining foreign reserves. Investors must prepare for continued liquidity tightening as the ''Warsh effect'' begins to ripple through global bond markets.

The AI Bubble Burst or Healthy Correction?

The tech-heavy Nasdaq is grappling with what many are calling a ''Squid Game'' for AI stocks. While Amazon and Apple reported resilient cloud and AI growth, others like Reddit and Roblox saw massive selloffs. The implosion of the Situational Awareness hedge fund serves as a stark warning of the risks embedded in leveraged AI bets. This volatility is causing a shift in capital toward safer havens in the APAC region, though emerging markets remain sensitive to any sudden withdrawal of global liquidity.

Geopolitical Risk and Energy Market Chaos

The conflict in West Asia, involving Iran and the US, has tightened blockades and sent oil prices into a tailspin of volatility. While Big Oil companies like Chevron and Exxon are reaping short-term rewards, the broader impact on global supply chains is inflationary. Vietnamese energy and logistics stocks may see short-term speculative interest, but the overarching sentiment remains one of caution as geopolitical fault lines deepen.

Investment Strategy: Rung Lac or Buy?

The current market state is one of ''Psychological Shaking'' (Rung lac tam ly). We recommend a defensive posture: holding cash and high-dividend compounders while waiting for a durable ceasefire or a Fed pause. For the Vietnamese market, monitor FDI inflows and export data closely. This is not a time for aggressive speculation but rather for selective accumulation of undervalued assets during deep corrections.
